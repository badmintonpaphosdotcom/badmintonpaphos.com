## General Rules

Welcome to Proodeftikos Badminton Club! Please follow these guidelines to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

### 1. Safety & Court Area

- Non-marking indoor court shoes required.
- Keep a safe distance from court lines. Do not walk near active courts.
- Active court: Shuttle in play or players on court for a match.

### 2. Equipment & Shuttle Handling

- Beginner players must use plastic shuttles until proper handling is mastered.
- Never strike feather shuttles directly on the feathers; hit only the cork base.

### 3. Onboarding

- Before their first game, new members must complete registration, receive a rule orientation, and sign a liability waiver.

### 4. Respect & Conduct

- Focus on constructive feedback. No negative criticism.
- No swearing directed at individuals.
- Give best effort to win.
- High-five open-handed post-match.

### 5. Operations

- No personal items on seating surfaces.
- Follow the rotation system for fair court access.
